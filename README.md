## 1. Executive Summary & City Rationale

### Why Challenge B & Why Bengaluru?
In college festivals, the binding constraint is **cash collected in the bank before the festival**, not signed MoU face value or in-kind barter. Selecting **Bengaluru** directly optimizes cash velocity:
1. **Shortest Corporate Approval Cycles:** Bengaluru hosts India's highest concentration of Series A–C D2C, Consumer Tech, Mobility/EV, and F&B startups (HSR, Indiranagar, Koramangala), where Founders and Heads of Growth approve ₹75k–₹3.0L campus activations in **10–18 days**—avoiding the 45–90 day enterprise procurement cycles typical of legacy conglomerates in Mumbai/NCR that push receivables past the festival.
2. **Direct Historical Category Fit:** In the provided `Sponsor Dataset.xlsx`, four startup-heavy verticals—**Consumer Products (₹4.50L), Mobility (₹4.45L), Food & Beverage (₹3.90L), and Personal Care (₹2.00L)**—generated **74.3% of all confirmed cash revenue**.

## 2. Historical Baseline Audit (`Sponsor Dataset.xlsx`)

Before modeling 2026 targets, all 12 sheets of `Sponsor Dataset.xlsx` were cleaned and audited:
* **Deduplication:** Removed exact import-retry duplicates (3 in `Sponsor interactions`, 1 in `Brand qualification`, 2 in `Opportunity history`) and resolved latest point-in-time states per brand and opportunity.

### Four Critical Bottlenecks Discovered
1. **Warm Handoff Leakage:** 18 qualified brands—including **12 from 100%-converting warm channels (`SC04` and `SC05`)**—never had an opportunity opened.
2. **Ownership Imbalance:** Kabir Shah owned **126 prospects (70%)** but **0 opportunities**, while Riya Kapoor drove **53.8% of cash** (₹10.75L, 100% cash) and Nisha Rao absorbed all 4 in-kind deals (₹2.40L).
3. **14-Day Velocity Cliff:** Won deals closed in a median **16.1 days** (6.0d touch→opp, 10.1d opp→confirmed) vs. **24.1 days** for lost deals. **₹14.40L (26 deals)** sat stalled at event start, including **₹7.20L in `negotiating`**.
4. **Reported Face Value ≠ Cash Collected:** The dataset explicitly notes `confirmed_value_inr` is reported face value, not bank receipt, and includes ₹2.40L of `in_kind` value.

## 4. Prioritization Framework: Cash-Adjusted RICE

$$\text{RICE} = \frac{\text{Reach (Qualified Cash Brands)} \times \text{Impact (Pre-Fest Cash Tier: } 0.25\text{–}3.0\text{)} \times \text{Confidence (2025 Evidence)}}{\text{Effort (Rep-Weeks out of 40 Available)}}$$

* **Double Down (Bets #1, #2, #3):** Consume 30 rep-weeks (75% capacity) and ₹1.05L spend; directly address the highest-leverage verified constraints (cash collection lag, warm intro volume, warm handoff leakage, and creator-led D2C conversion).
* **Test / Stage (Bet #4 — Self-Serve ROI Portal):** Staged with a ₹10k initial test gate; remaining ₹20k unlocks only if CPQL < ₹2,500 and Form→Meeting ≥ 65%.
* **Explicitly Reject / Kill (Bet #5 — High-Volume Cold Email & LinkedIn Blasts):** Scored **0.50** RICE. Disproven by 2025 data (322 attempts → ₹0 closed). Eliminating it frees 18 rep-weeks for high-intent warm closings.

## 5. Growth Center Architecture & AI Copilot Design

* **Files Included:**

  * `campusfest_growth_center.html` — Single-file, zero-build-step interactive web application (HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript with `localStorage` persistence).

* **How to Run Locally:** Open `campusfest_growth_center.html` directly in Chrome, Edge, or Safari—no server or npm install required.
* **How to Deploy to Vercel / Netlify:** Place `campusfest_growth_center.html` as `index.html` in a GitHub repository and deploy as a static site (0 build config).
* **Optional Live Gemini API Mode:** Click **⚙ AI Engine Settings** in the sidebar and paste a free Google AI Studio key (`gemini-2.5-flash`). Without a key, the app runs its built-in **Deterministic Live-State Analytics Engine** out of the box.

### Data Persistence & Live Recalculation
Every edit—changing an opportunity's stage, entering verified Pre-Fest Bank Cash Collected, editing campaign budgets/qualified counts, converting leaked brands, or creating/completing tasks—writes immediately to `localStorage` and triggers `computeAnalytics()`, updating all KPI cards, funnel charts, guardrail alerts, and AI Copilot answers simultaneously. Use **↺ Reset 2025 Baseline** in the sidebar at any time to restore the exact original dataset.

## 7. Tools Used & Known Limitations

* **Tools Used:** Python (`pandas`, `matplotlib`) for auditing and verifying `Sponsor Dataset.xlsx`; vanilla HTML/CSS/JS for the standalone Growth Center app; optional Google AI Studio (`gemini-2.5-flash`) REST integration for natural-language synthesis.
* **Known Limitations:**
  1. **Browser-Local Persistence:** State persists in `localStorage` for zero-setup portability rather than a multi-user cloud database (easily portable to Supabase by swapping `loadState`/`persistState`).
  2. **2025 Entity Representation:** All 58 opportunities and 6 campaigns mathematically reconcile to the exact rupee and deal counts of `Sponsor Dataset.xlsx` (including Beardo, Mosaic Mobility, Maple Devices, and all 4 team owners), while individual non-anchor brand names on anonymized rows are simulated to make the interactive CRM realistic and legible.
  3. **Local Engine NLP Scope:** Without a Gemini API key, the deterministic fallback handles structured intents, brand lookups, live math, follow-ups, and trap queries reliably; connecting a free Gemini API key unlocks fully arbitrary free-form Q&A while preserving live-state JSON grounding.
